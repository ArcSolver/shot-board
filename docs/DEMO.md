# Two-minute walkthrough

1. **0:00–0:20 — Set the scene.** Show the one-line scene and character/set bible. Change a lens and lighting chip; explain that movement is still-image composition intent.
2. **0:20–0:35 — Find the coverage.** Build six cuts, open the editable list and refine one prompt.
3. **0:35–0:50 — Action.** Queue two takes per cut. Show the real queue, engine state and elapsed time. For a short recording, visibly cut forward or label a time-lapse; do not imply instant rendering.
4. **0:50–1:20 — Make the selects.** Open a completed board. Compare the two takes side by side. Pick one and lock its seed. Show that the look is related while faces/props and exact framing can still vary.
5. **1:20–1:40 — Keep the recipe.** Pick the remaining cuts and export. Show the selected stills, ComfyUI workflows and manifest's checkpoint hash, seed and sampler settings.
6. **1:40–1:55 — Leave a door open.** Show Reframe's experimental coming-soon response. Mention close-up/small parallax as future work, with no claim of implemented geometry editing.

The included example can be imported and browsed without running diffusion again. No deployment or hosted service is needed for this walkthrough.
