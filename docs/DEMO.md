# Demo walkthrough

## Follow the same path yourself

Start the app as in [Try it without a GPU](../README.md#try-it-without-a-gpu).

1. On the home page, open **The hangar** example and choose **Compare & pick** on 03 Close-up to read its Fail verdict and note.
2. Back on the home page, choose **Explore the board** on the greenhouse.
3. Open **Compare & pick** on 04 Over shoulder. Compare the SDXL take with the selected Qwen edit. Save a verdict and a note.
4. Click **Storyboard**, switch to Portrait and click **Download PNG**. Then click **Export ZIP** and open `manifest.json` to see the recipes and reviews.
