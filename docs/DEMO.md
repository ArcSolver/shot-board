# Demo walkthrough

The demo video follows one scene from a sentence to a storyboard. You can take the same path in the app.

## With a GPU worker

Start the app as in [Run it locally](../README.md#run-it-locally).

1. On the home page, choose **New scene**. Type the scene or press **Use example**, then **Build shot list**.
2. Choose **Render board**. Takes arrive cut by cut.
3. Open **Compare & pick** on 02 Medium. **Pick** a take, open **Seed, hero & more takes** and choose **Use selected as hero**.
4. Back on the board, choose **Render reference edits**. The other cuts are re-rendered from the hero.
5. Click **Storyboard**, switch to Portrait and click **Download PNG**. Then click **Export ZIP**.

## Without a GPU

Start the app as in [Try it without a GPU](../README.md#try-it-without-a-gpu).

1. On the home page, choose **Explore the board**. The greenhouse board opens with its hero and reference edits in place.
2. Open **Compare & pick** on 02 Medium to see the eight SDXL takes and the hero pick.
3. Open **Compare & pick** on 01 Establishing to compare the SDXL take with the Qwen edits from the hero.
4. Click **Storyboard**, switch to Portrait and click **Download PNG**. Then click **Export ZIP** and open `manifest.json` to see the recipes and reviews.
